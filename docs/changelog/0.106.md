# Canic 0.106 Detailed Changelog

## [0.106.0] - 2026-08-20 - Fleet Estate Qualification Baseline

### 🔧 Changed

- Freezes published `v0.105.0` as the immutable direct predecessor while
  retaining the unchanged 0.104 timer-consumer, async-recovery and stable-state
  authority across current Root pool paths.
- Accepts repository-local protocol `canic-0.106-q3q4-v1`, including separate
  warm-ups, disposable 1/8/16/32 creation and reset cohorts, three measured
  repetitions, a one-lane mainnet confirmation, exact admission/deadline/
  censoring rules and no cross-network or causal aggregation.
- Freezes a seven-day standby horizon with six-hour observations, checked burn
  accounting, an explicit safety-margin formula, a two-attempt recovery reserve
  and workload-qualified balance formulae. These remain dated inputs for 0.109,
  not universal balance requirements.
- Pins the installed reset workload to its predecessor source, canonical build
  identities, initialized memory observations, raw Wasm size and SHA-256. Any
  identity drift invalidates the fixture rather than selecting a replacement.
- Records the deployed Cycles Ledger v1.0.6 interface/source authority,
  creation and withdrawal fees, nanosecond deduplication identity, time-window
  edges, duplicate-without-Principal uncertainty and exact-request retry
  contract separately from every pending platform observation.
- Freezes proposed disposable and minimal-mainnet operation, physical-asset,
  concurrency, fee/refund, unresolved-reserve and funded-exposure ceilings.
  The proposal grants no network, identity, cycle-spend or asset-disposition
  authority.

### 📊 Audit

- The complete current Root pool inventory covers stable IDs and encodings,
  creation and reset state, scans, native timer custody, snapshot ownership,
  receipts, cost records and lifecycle reachability against the accepted 0.104
  boundary.
- The exact empty-topology host plan rejects at `EmptyRootAdmissions`. B1
  accepts that as a 0.109-owned production blocker rather than fabricating a
  PocketIC activation path.
- Four current-state constraints are assigned to 0.109 without repair in this
  evidence-only line: unbounded failure text, a structurally narrow 64-byte
  handoff-receipt bound with a 67-byte counterexample, unbounded terminal
  receipt retention and the absence of a canonical receipt-map snapshot
  payload.
- The proposed disposable envelope permits at most 172 physical assets,
  concurrency 32 and `2,590,086,000,000,000` cycles of funded exposure. The
  minimal mainnet envelope permits at most four assets, concurrency one and
  `70,002,000,000,000` cycles of funded exposure. These are authorization
  ceilings, not observed costs.
- Repository-local qualification caused zero remote Canister creation, cycle
  transfer, controller change, mainnet call or sibling-repository mutation.
- No production configuration, stable record, Candid method, CLI command,
  timer owner, package version or shipped runtime behavior is added by the
  qualification harness.
- Every package already present in the predecessor lockfile retains its exact
  version. The only graph additions are a dev-only direct PocketIC edge for
  unpublished test support and the separately classified, unpublished 0.108
  B1 probe; neither enters a shipped role or the 0.106 protocol.

### 🧪 Testing

- A test-only Cycles Ledger boundary proves ordinary Root creation and exact
  same-request recovery after an unresolved duplicate returns the original
  Principal. Wrong Root, Subnet, amount, timestamp and creation bindings are
  rejected.
- Creation preflights pass cohort widths 1, 8, 16 and 32 with every lane
  submitted before collection, one independent unresolved lane, exact retry,
  unique returned Principals and rejection of the first excess lane.
- Empty-control and exact-workload reset preflights pass the same cohort widths
  with separately accounted setup top-ups, exact 5T starting balances and every
  terminal asset running, Root-only, on the selected Subnet and uninstalled.
- Controller qualification observes the exact
  `[source] -> [source, destination] -> [destination]` transition. Missing or
  contradictory routing evidence rejects before the first mutation.
- A terminal package/source guard keeps the qualification Canisters unique,
  unpublished dependency leaves under `canisters/test` and absent from shipped
  role configuration. Qualification helpers and the pinned PocketIC edge remain
  test/dev-only.
- The release-tree classification separates the co-delivered 0.108 B1 probe,
  its PocketIC journey and the settled successor renumbering from 0.106 B1/B2,
  preserving their test/documentation-only reachability without presenting
  them as platform qualification.
- Focused host, control-plane, PocketIC, Clippy, metadata, document-semantics,
  changelog and whitespace checks pass. Complete release validation remains
  maintainer-owned.

### 📝 Summary

- Repository-local B1 was accepted on 2026-08-20 and no local B1 work remains.
  B2 is still held pending a separate exact authorization naming the disposable
  and mainnet networks, signing/funding identities and terminal asset
  disposition. This changelog remains an open draft and cannot become a
  published 0.106 release until those B2 results are executed, reconciled and
  accepted; no B2 empirical row or completed 0.109 handoff is claimed here.
- Scheduled 0.107 deployment readiness and the renumbered 0.108 root-funding
  proof may consume accepted B1 without waiting for B2. Mutating 0.109 Fleet-
  estate work remains gated on the separately accepted B2 qualification.
