## Authority And Effect

on 2026-08-24. Functional 0.109 B1-B7 completion, a passing focused test,
generic continuation wording or the prior scheduled status of 0.110 cannot

external-repository mutation or broad validation. The maintainer-owned 0.109
release and separately authorized downstream adoption are B8 prerequisites;
neither closes the 0.109 minor or unblocks 0.110. B9 remediation must not begin
until B8 closes.

One admission-policy change currently spans 204 tracked/new paths and repeats

end-to-end admission authority/transition map; remove or consolidate every
confirmed competing decision; and retain duplicated boundary data only with an
explicit passive-transport or persistence reason.

### CANIC-109-STRUCTURE-002 — Mature Cut Points Remain In Gravity-Well Modules

```text
finding_class: operational_risk
severity: P2
confidence: confirmed
finding_status: accepted
owner: component Registry and Fleet Coordinator module topology
affected_surfaces: control-plane ops, control-plane workflow, tests
```

The three named modules contain established allocation, installation,
directory, draining, removal, funding, Registry and admission responsibilities.
These are no longer hypothetical early abstractions; their state and workflow
cut points are already present. Keeping them in multi-thousand-line modules
makes unrelated changes share imports, private helpers and review context.

Required disposition: apply Tier 2 finding-backed Module Surface Hardening.
Decompose along existing authority/lifecycle seams in small behavior-preserving
slices, or retain an individual block only with a named owner, an invariant
reason and a concrete shrink trigger. The outcome must reduce mixed
responsibility and navigation span; moving lines without improving ownership
does not close the finding.

## Verdict

0.109's functional ingress-admission implementation is not rejected. Its
accumulated product, module, documentation, verification and roadmap
complexity is rejected as the baseline for beginning 0.110.

Keep 0.109 open. First publish 0.109.2, review and resume the retained
downstream operation, and qualify the resulting operator loop through B8; then
simplify it and the inherited gravity wells in B9, rerun the
canonical methods on an immutable source and obtain an accepted passing
verdict, then complete B10's separate managed-App qualification support and the
human closeout. Until then, 0.110 is not authorized.
