## Executive Summary

`v0.109.34` is the latest immutable 0.109 tag and its nine published crates are
version- and source-consistent with the tag. The original admission authority,
local projection, convergence, stable-bound, hard-cut, and public testing-facade
contracts remain substantially implemented. The current tag also passes the
repository's complete local validation gate when the RustSec advisory fetch is
allowed, and the canonical B9 measurement scripts remain deterministic.

Closeout nevertheless fails for three independent reasons:

1. **CANIC-118 is a confirmed current-runtime blocker.** A normal fresh Fleet
   reaches an issued, receipt-backed Root-only pool `Create`, but the operator
   cannot obtain the full status shape that `v0.109.34` requires before the Root
   is installed. ICP CLI correctly falls back to public status, while Canic
   rejects that response as malformed. The exact operation remains safe and
   resumable, but fresh convergence cannot complete.
2. **The accepted B9 closeout condition regressed on the actual closeout tag.**
   The three gravity-well parents remain at their accepted sizes and no second
   authority was found, but the active handoff is 354 lines, above the accepted
   sub-250-line condition. The accepted `v0.109.32` verdict therefore cannot be
   inherited unchanged by `v0.109.34`.
3. **Active release documents contradict immutable reality.** The tag's current
   status says development begins from `v0.109.33`, repeatedly calls published
   `v0.109.34` a draft, and says B10 downstream adoption is pending. The design
   and status trackers still show B2-B7 as `Ready` and B10 removal as pending,
   although the inspected downstream has adopted `canic::testing` and removed
   the private dependency/adapter ownership. These are active closeout claims,
   not safely fenced historical prose.

The maintainer must not accept 0.109 closeout or promote 0.110 from this report.
The narrow next action is to publish and qualify a correction for CANIC-118,
reconcile active 0.109 release truth, restore or explicitly re-accept the B9
handoff bound, then request a fresh closeout audit on that immutable tag.
