## Why This Line Changed

The earlier tracked 0.110 revision put build acceleration, operator inventory,
status cleanup and stateful retirement ahead of the strongest measured Wasm
work. It also measured defined functions without requiring final function
headroom. That structure could spend the reserve it was supposed to create.

The restored direction follows the actual evidence:

1. Binaryen has already produced its material general-purpose reduction;
2. the remaining downstream pressure is both code-section size and the exact
   function quantity enforced by the replica validator;
3. shallow post-`v0.109.28` attribution found large overlapping
   Serde/Ciborium reachability around Canic stable records; and
4. global storage registration can make role-inapplicable records, codecs and
   recovery work reachable in otherwise small actors; and
5. non-storage macro expansions can retain all-role endpoints, Candid roots,
   providers, timers and control-plane dispatch even when the consuming actor
   does not expose those capabilities; and
6. a repository-owned endpoint-heavy fixture cannot prove that the real Toko
   `project_instance` combination has usable network headroom.

The roughly 725 KiB Canic-associated Ciborium total is routing evidence, not a
savings forecast. Activation, authorization and other symbol families overlap,
and shared machinery may remain after one family is removed. Controlled
ablation builds and fresh marginal measurements therefore own every
implementation decision.

## Decision

0.110 is a zero-capability contraction release:

1. Every retained runtime capability has one owner, one compiled projection
   and one measured inclusive and marginal cost under a named immutable build.
   Shared artifact costs are never treated as additive.
2. Every consuming actor generates an exact role/capability-specific runtime
   graph, including direct initialization, restore and recovery calls. It
   retains no exhaustive all-role dispatcher or executable callback registry.
3. Stable TLS is lazy. Defining a stable domain in `canic-core` does not make
   its record and serializer reachable in every role.
4. Activation and authorization persistence use capability-owned concrete
   records instead of whole-world enums or aggregates.
5. A stable codec changes only after current marginal evidence justifies it.
6. Exported endpoints remain thin marshalling/authentication/delegation
   boundaries; repeated work is shared only when optimized artifact evidence
   proves that LLVM did not recreate the duplication.
7. Roles do not compile unused command, metric, provisioning, configuration,
   diagnostic or recovery implementations.
8. Each runtime batch ends with a fresh byte/function report and an explicit
   stop, continue or retarget decision.
9. Existing security, Candid, status, recovery and operator semantics remain
   intact except for the pre-1.0 stable-codec hard cut expressly selected by
   measured evidence.
10. Importing or splitting a Rust macro receives no contraction credit by
    itself. The accepted evidence is what each expansion references and what
    the final optimized role artifact proves absent.
11. The exact frozen Toko `project_instance` is a binding downstream canary,
    not a savings-attribution source or a new Canic production dependency.

## Non-Goals

0.110 does not:

- add retirement, cycle transfer, estates, reserve Fleets or another lifecycle
  protocol;
- redesign the status API or add a semantic-version inventory;
- add a runtime Observatory, renderer or telemetry service;
- add rolling, mixed-version or cross-release operation;
- preserve predecessor stable bytes through a decoder or migration lane;
- replace chain-key ECDSA, Root authentication or the external verification
  model;
- optimize pagination, timers or generic families ahead of stronger current
  evidence;
- weaken Candid, exports, features, provenance, deterministic gzip, release
  finalization or exact module-hash authority;
- move unresolved 0.109 work into this minor; or
- modify Toko Miner, IcyDB or another repository.

## B1: Immutable Baseline And Absolute Budgets

B1 freezes one immutable post-0.109 source, toolchain, feature and optimizer
baseline. It owns two distinct qualification lanes:

1. every canonical Canic role and one repository-owned endpoint-heavy managed
   fixture; and
2. the exact read-only Toko `project_instance` canary described below.

For every artifact, record:

- code-section bytes;
- total Wasm bytes;
- deterministic compressed artifact bytes;
- the exact function count reported by a frozen replica-equivalent validator;
- defined-function count;
- table elements and other indirect-function entries;
- representative instruction counts;
- a named post-`-Oz` Canic monomorphization report;
- exports, Candid and selected capabilities; and
- clean/warm release-build time, peak RSS and process/thread high-water marks.

B1 also records the dated authoritative network limits for code-section bytes,
total module bytes and declared functions. The acceptance measurement uses a
frozen replica-equivalent validator; it does not infer the enforced count from
code-section bodies. Defined functions remain a separate attribution metric.
Completion is defined against the final artifact:

The frozen interpretation follows the
[IC WebAssembly module requirements](https://docs.internetcomputer.org/references/ic-interface-spec/canister-interface/),
which state the function limit in terms of functions declared by the module.

```text
code_headroom =
    network_code_section_limit - final_code_section_bytes

function_headroom =
    network_declared_function_limit - final_validator_function_count

required_function_reserve =
    ceil(network_declared_function_limit * 5 / 100)

required_code_reserve =
    ceil(network_code_section_limit * 5 / 100)
```

`final_validator_function_count` is the exact quantity counted by the frozen
replica validator for its declared-function limit. B1 records the validator
version, configuration and result so a source-level or parser-specific
definition cannot silently replace that gate.

The worst qualified artifact must satisfy all independent gates:

```text
code_headroom >= required_code_reserve
function_headroom >= required_function_reserve
final_total_module_bytes < network_total_module_limit
```

Under the retained 10 MiB code-section limit, the binding 5% reserve is
512 KiB. A 1 MiB code-section reserve is preferred. Reduction from a failing
baseline is useful attribution but never completion evidence. If a network
limit or toolchain changes, the baseline and accepted limits must be frozen
again.

Representative workloads receive one explicit maximum 1% instruction
regression allowance. B1 may select a smaller allowance; later batches cannot
silently expand it.

### Binding Toko Project-Instance Canary

B1 freezes the exact Toko `project_instance` application snapshot as an
immutable downstream canary. Its manifest records at least:

- the Toko source commit and clean-source assertion;
- the baseline and candidate `Cargo.lock` digests and complete resolved
  Canic/IcyDB dependency graphs;
- the exact Cargo features, Rust toolchain, linker, Binaryen/finalizer version
  and optimizer arguments;
- the generated Candid digest and exact exported-method count;
- code-section, total and deterministic compressed bytes;
- validator-counted and defined functions plus table/indirect entries;
- representative instruction measurements; and
- the exact Canic source or immutable package set substituted for each build.

The supplied planning observation reports 10,275,629 code-section bytes,
268 exported Candid methods and only 210,131 bytes below the retained 10 MiB
limit. Those numbers are routing input until B1 attaches the complete immutable
manifest and reproduces them. At that size, reaching the binding 512 KiB
reserve requires a 314,157-byte reduction. Passing a smaller repository fixture
or merely shrinking this baseline cannot substitute for the final absolute
gate.

B1 retains an explicit capability matrix for the canary. The frozen fixture
must contain and exercise, or structurally prove reachable where an instruction
journey is inapplicable, this combined surface:

| Capability family | Frozen canary requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Authentication | Delegated-token verification and Root-signature verification are both selected. |
| Blob economics | Blob storage and billing are selected together. |
| Public surface | The generated Candid digest and all 268 exported methods match the frozen snapshot. |
| Generated data access | The snapshot's complete IcyDB-generated entity, query and serialization graph remains selected. |
| Lifecycle | Initialization, restoration, activation and authorized reinstall paths are represented. |
| Operations | Status, metrics, timers and applicable same-release recovery paths are represented. |

Only the Canic source/package input may vary between the paired predecessor and
candidate canary builds. Toko application source, IcyDB source, features and
build configuration remain byte-identical. Both lockfiles are frozen, and
their retained diff may contain only the named Canic package source/version/
checksum changes required by the candidate. This canary is read-only
qualification: 0.110 gains no authority to edit Toko Miner or IcyDB.

### Differential Attribution

From the same immutable source and build configuration, B1 performs controlled
one-switch ablation or paired-projection builds for at least:

1. the current baseline;
2. global stable-storage registration disabled;
3. the activation record/codec family excluded;
4. the authorization record/codec family excluded;
5. the relevant CBOR family replaced by a bounded measurement stub;
6. the unconditional recovery-dispatch family excluded;
7. current macro expansion compared with exact role/capability expansion;
8. endpoint Candid type construction excluded;
9. Candid type-documentation generation excluded;
10. Candid serialization and transparent-newtype adapters excluded;
11. payload-limited async adapters excluded;
12. metrics-provider expansion excluded;
13. configuration and provisioning-provider expansion excluded;
14. command-provider expansion excluded;
15. timer and watchdog-provider expansion excluded;
16. the relevant status-projection family excluded;
17. one controlled Canic generic-instantiation cohort from one through `N`
    equivalent type instantiations; and
18. the immutable predecessor compared with the current candidate after the
    complete temporary pool Ledger recovery family was deleted, including its
    generated helper, Store publication, Root runtime surface and host
    planning/execution path.

Every experiment records the complete artifact vector above. An experiment
that cannot execute a workload records that absence and cannot support an
instruction-parity or savings claim for that path. Measurement switches and
stubs never become published runtime features.

Shallow totals, ablation deltas and later marginal reductions may overlap.
They are never added into a recoverable-byte forecast.

### Generic-Instantiation Cohort

B1 selects at least one Canic-owned generic family reached by a canonical
generated surface and freezes an equivalent nominal-type cohort of size `N`.
`N >= 2` is accepted before measurement from representative current Canic
role/capability demand; the downstream 11-entity example does not choose it.

Using the same source, toolchain, features, optimizer and generated capability
surface, B1 builds the cohort with `1..=N` instantiations. Adding type `k` may
change only the nominal type instantiation under test. For every build, record
the complete artifact vector and report both:

- the inclusive cost at each cohort size; and
- the marginal code-section bytes, validator-counted functions, defined
  functions and table/indirect entries from `k - 1` to `k`.

The retained named report is produced from the canonical final artifact after
Binaryen `-Oz`. It maps remaining Canic-owned generic families to fully
qualified functions, concrete type arguments, specialized machine-code bodies
and their inclusive/marginal artifact evidence. Shared code, optimizer folding
and nonlinear deltas are explicit; no average per-type slope is multiplied
into a savings forecast.

The cohort is an attribution fixture, not a new runtime capability. If earlier
B2-B4 cuts remove or materially change the selected family, that batch refreshes
the named post-`-Oz` report and explicitly retains, retargets or stops the
cohort before later work proceeds.

### Batch Measurement Gate

Every B2-B4 batch stops before later implementation and records:

- all canonical role builds and the endpoint-heavy fixture;
- code, total and compressed bytes;
- validator-counted and defined functions plus table/indirect entries;
- representative instruction deltas;
- the fresh named post-`-Oz` monomorphization report when generic fanout
  changes;
- fresh marginal attribution against the immediately preceding immutable
  build; and
- an explicit stop, continue or retarget decision.

The frozen Toko canary is rebuilt at B1 and B5. A B2-B4 checkpoint also rebuilds
it whenever that batch changes a Canic surface reached by the capability
matrix; otherwise the checkpoint retains exact optimized-artifact evidence that
the changed surface is unreachable from the canary.

B2-B4 are not automatically cumulative. B3 proceeds only when residual
record/codec attribution and independent complexity benefit justify it. B4 is
mandatory whenever the generated-surface inventory still identifies a
role-inapplicable endpoint, provider, serializer, timer, recovery or
control-plane implementation in a canonical role or the frozen canary.
Headroom alone cannot waive that absence criterion. An individual B4 cut may
stop or retarget only after optimized evidence proves the suspected machinery
absent, irreducibly shared or more expensive to remove within the frozen
allowance.

## B2: Role-Selected Storage Reachability

B2 replaces global eager stable-store registration with generated direct role
wiring in the consuming actor. The starting boundaries include
`canic-core/src/memory_macros.rs` and
`canic-core/src/storage/stable/mod.rs`.

Generated code uses closed calls such as:

```rust
fn initialize_selected_storage() {
    initialize_activation();
    initialize_local_auth();
}
```

The role expansion must:

1. make stable TLS lazy by default;
2. remove constructor-driven executable store registration;
3. retain global memory-ID reservations as data-only metadata;
4. touch only stores selected by the consuming role/capability graph;
5. emit direct role-specific initialization, restore and same-release recovery
   calls; and
6. keep unselected record, serializer and recovery implementations out of the
   consuming compilation graph.

Executable selection must not use slices of function pointers, trait objects,
erased callbacks or another indirect registry. Data-only memory declarations
may remain declarative.

Artifact ratchets prove, for example, that an ordinary role does not contain
Root issuer state, Wasm Store records, Fleet-admission storage, sharding
storage or automatic-top-up recovery unless its exact role selects them.

## B3: Capability-Owned Records And Conditional Codecs

After B2 remeasurement, B3 may split the measured whole-world record families.
It must not replace an aggregate with an enum whose variants still instantiate
every codec.

Activation persistence becomes capability-owned concrete records, such as:

- ordinary activation;
- Root activation; and
- Wasm Store activation.

Bootstrap validates only the selected record and projects its compact phase
into transient runtime state. The projection is a cache reconstructed after
restart, never a second authority. A transition updates that cache only after
the corresponding durable transition commits successfully.

Authorization persistence separates at least:

- local application authorization and session/replay state;
- delegated-token issuer state;
- verifier/cache state; and
- Root issuer, renewal and chain-key state.

An ordinary application role must not instantiate Root renewal, chain-key
batch or issuer-policy codecs merely because another role supports them.

### Positional Codec Hard Cut

Only a record family that remains materially attributable after the preceding
remeasurement may replace named-map CBOR. Each selected stable wire type is
separate from its domain record and public DTO and has:

- a distinct magic/prefix that legacy bytes cannot match accidentally;
- codec version 1 separate from logical record-schema version 1;
- positional fields and numeric enum discriminants;
- fixed endianness for multibyte values;
- one canonical optional-field representation;
- exact maximum lengths checked before allocation;
- an explicit maximum encoded size;
- rejection of trailing bytes; and
- independently retained golden vectors not generated by the tested encoder.

No predecessor decoder, compatibility alias or fallback remains. The host and
actor lifecycle reject an attempted predecessor upgrade before interpreting
stable state or performing another effect. Direct qualification proves that
the rejected upgrade leaves code, stable state, authority and workflow effects
unchanged, and that only the authorized reinstall path can cross the hard cut.

B1 must inventory every state domain deliberately destroyed by the hard cut
and name how fresh Root, authentication, topology and application authority is
reconstructed after reinstall. Missing reconstruction authority blocks the
codec change.

### Reinstall-Only Release Enforcement

The hard cut is executable release policy, not narrative guidance. Governed
0.110 release metadata records a machine-readable reinstall-only transition
mode. Host planning and release qualification must reject an ordinary upgrade,
adoption or mixed-version transition for an already installed stateful Fleet
before issuing any install, stop, controller, funding or other platform effect.

Only the separately authorized reinstall path may cross the release boundary.
It must first prove the reviewed destroyed-state inventory, reconstruction
authority, controller authority and cycle-conservation plan. Direct negative
qualification records the typed pre-effect rejection and unchanged module,
stable state, controller, cycle and workflow observations. No generic
`upgrade` command, mode inference or fallback may silently turn the hard cut
into an upgrade attempt.

## B4: Endpoint, Recovery And Role-Capability Pruning

The endpoint audit maps every export to transport decoding, authentication,
admission, workflow delegation, reply encoding and diagnostic conversion.
Repeated work moves behind shared non-generic functions only when residual
evidence justifies it.

Source extraction is not proof. Acceptance requires the optimized artifact to
show:

- fewer duplicate machine-code bodies;
- lower defined and validator-counted function totals;
- no unexpected table or indirect-dispatch increase;
- instruction deltas inside the frozen allowance; and
- retained absolute byte and function reserves.

Selective `#[inline(never)]` may be tested, but it remains only when the final
optimized Wasm proves the desired size/function/instruction result.

Recovery dispatch is generated as closed direct role code. Ordinary,
automatic-top-up, delegated-token issuer and Root recovery paths do not share
one unconditional dispatcher. Only Root may reference Root issuer renewal,
chain-key batch preparation and Root installation recovery.

B4 also removes role-inapplicable metrics, commands, full configuration,
provisioning and diagnostics. Smaller generic cuts—async adapters, access
expression construction, Candid type documentation or transparent newtype
serialization—proceed only when fresh residual evidence justifies them.

Existing typed status contracts and Fleet Ensure ownership remain unchanged.

### Pool Ledger Recovery Hard Cut

The maintainer retired `pool_ledger_recovery` from the current contract in
published `v0.110.3`. This supersedes the earlier conditional B4 deletion plan;
it does not claim that the former incident-specific gate became a general Fleet
capability or that old operation evidence remains supported.

The complete feature is deleted in one current-contract cut:

- helper source, generated artifact and release-build role;
- Wasm Store template/publication entry;
- Root workflow, durable progress/receipt state, status and command endpoint;
- boundary DTOs and Candid reachability;
- host observation, planning, policy and apply support; and
- feature-specific tests, fixtures and CI ownership.

No migration, compatibility reader, alias, fallback or product protocol v2 is
permitted. This is a Canic deletion only. Current Fleet Ensure funding targets
native canister balances and never creates the accidental Ledger-account state
that motivated the helper. An externally created Ledger-account credit remains
outside the desired-state contract; 0.110 neither changes downstream code nor
treats the helper's reported roughly 195 KiB compressed artifact as part of an
unrelated frontend upload or as predicted recoverable code-section bytes.

## Macro And Generated-Surface Contraction

B1 inventories, B2 contracts the storage/lifecycle portion of, and B4 closes
the remaining public and internal macro expansions that contribute runtime
reachability. Importing a Rust macro does not itself add runtime Wasm; the
functions, types, statics, dispatchers and serializers referenced by its
expansion determine the reachable graph. The inventory includes at least:

- `start!` and lifecycle expansion;
- memory registration and stable-storage selection;
- role endpoint generation;
- authentication and admission wrappers;
- status and Candid type-generation roots;
- metrics-provider expansion;
- command, configuration and provisioning expansion;
- timer and watchdog expansion; and
- recovery dispatch for Coordinator, Root, Wasm Store and ordinary roles.

For each macro and consuming actor, the retained inventory names the validated
role/capability input and every generated endpoint, function, type, static,
provider, dispatcher, serializer, timer and recovery root that may contribute
runtime code. Compile-only imports and declarations are distinguished from
expanded references that enter the actor's reachability graph.

Each actor expansion may generate only:

- endpoints exported by that actor;
- authentication and admission paths used by those endpoints;
- selected storage initialization, restoration and same-release recovery;
- selected timer and recovery dispatch;
- selected metrics, command, configuration and provisioning providers; and
- Candid types, construction and serialization reachable from the actor's
  exact public surface.

Generated code must not reference:

- an exhaustive all-role runtime dispatcher;
- an enum, tuple or provider aggregate that instantiates every role;
- an executable registration inventory containing unused callbacks;
- Root, Coordinator or Wasm Store workflows from an ordinary actor;
- automatic-top-up, issuer-renewal or sharding machinery unless selected; or
- Candid type construction, documentation or serialization for an endpoint
  absent from the actor.

The implementation may split macros into role/capability-specific entry points
or retain one macro that expands selectively from an exact validated contract.
Source organization and expanded-source appearance are not completion
evidence. Each canonical role must prove optimized-artifact absence plus
differential byte, validator-function, defined-function and indirect-dispatch
evidence against its frozen baseline.

## Parallel Release-Build Work

Release-build acceleration may run alongside B2-B4 when it preserves
measurement integrity. Eligible work includes non-LTO declaration extraction,
canonical sidecar reuse, bounded per-role build context, compatible runtime
batching and measured LTO/codegen A/B experiments.

This work is not a release batch and receives no runtime-contraction credit
unless a final optimized artifact actually shrinks. A build change that alters
Wasm rebases subsequent attribution. Unfinished build acceleration is deferred
rather than delaying the byte/function decision.

## B5: Binding Downstream Qualification And Closeout

B5 rebuilds every canonical Canic artifact and the exact frozen Toko
`project_instance` canary twice from clean inputs. It retains deterministic
artifact digests, the complete byte/function/table/instruction vector, the
capability matrix and the structured reinstall-only rejection evidence.

The Toko canary must satisfy the same binding 5% code-section and
validator-function reserves, including at least 512 KiB of code-section
headroom under the retained 10 MiB limit. One MiB of downstream code-section
headroom remains preferred. A Toko successor snapshot may replace the B1
canary only through an explicit immutable rebaseline that retains a paired
predecessor build and proves that the capability matrix and endpoint scale did
not shrink. A moving or dirty downstream worktree cannot silently replace the
frozen canary.

If all accepted safe Canic cuts leave the canary below either binding reserve,
0.110 does not claim completion. B5 retains a downstream handoff that separates
the remaining Canic-owned, IcyDB-owned and Toko-application-owned optimized
families; records inclusive and marginal evidence without summing overlaps;
and names the exact residual byte, validator-function and instruction gap.
Canic then stops. Downstream owners may address their code under their own
authority and return an immutable successor canary, but 0.110 does not modify
their repositories, weaken authentication or spend the reserve on a new
runtime capability.

## Validation Policy

The line freezes a time, RSS, process/thread and case-count envelope. Prefer:

1. pure policy/state-transition tests;
2. stable/model codec, corruption and property tests;
3. deterministic host fixtures;
4. one representative PocketIC journey per distinct external-effect or
   cross-canister invariant; and
5. read-only downstream qualification.

Every retained PocketIC case names the invariant unavailable at a cheaper
layer. A new case replaces duplicate evidence or justifies a distinct
distributed invariant.

## Completion Criteria

0.110 is complete only when:

1. B1 retains reproducible controlled-ablation evidence without adding
   overlapping savings;
2. the final worst-case canonical artifact and frozen Toko canary each have at
   least 5% of the dated network code-section limit free—512 KiB under the
   retained 10 MiB limit—and at least 5% of the dated network declared-function
   limit free under the frozen replica-equivalent validator;
3. every artifact remains below the independent total-module limit;
4. frozen workloads remain inside the accepted instruction allowance;
5. every implemented batch records fresh marginal attribution and an explicit
   stop, continue or retarget decision;
6. roles initialize, restore and recover through generated direct calls with
   no executable function-pointer or erased-callback registry;
7. unselected stable domains, serializers, recovery workflows and role
   capabilities do not enter ordinary artifacts;
8. any selected codec rejects legacy/trailing/first-excess bytes and has
   independent golden vectors plus complete destroyed-state/reconstruction
   evidence;
9. endpoint extraction reduces optimized duplicate bodies and functions
   without unexplained indirect dispatch;
10. two clean release builds remain byte deterministic and preserve Candid,
    export, feature, provenance and finalizer gates;
